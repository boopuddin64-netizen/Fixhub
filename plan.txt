   State machine:

   - Phase 3 (Description): "Anything else happening?" The user can type, use voice note, or attach photos.
   - Phase 4 (Location): "Let's confirm where you'll take the phone." We render the `LocationSelector` inline or as a modal.
   - Phase 5 (Review): "Here's your repair request."
